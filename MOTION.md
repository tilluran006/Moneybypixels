# MoneyByPixels motion rules (reels)

**Idea: money becomes pixels.** One pool of orange squares runs through the whole reel and rearranges from scene to scene. In every scene each square means one stated unit (₹1,000, ₹1, one month), and the unit is written on screen.

- **Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, **15–20 s** (hard limit 22 s), at most 5 scenes: hook, 2–3 middle, verdict. Text stays clear of the bottom 320 px and right 150 px (Instagram UI).
- **Hook rule (learned from reel 1, 60% skip rate):** frame one carries the whole tension, fully on screen: the hero number at its final value and the line that explains it, ideally speaking to the viewer ("Paying only the minimum due?"). No fade-in and no count-up from zero in the hook; the loop ends on this same frame.
- **Hook checklist (the renderer enforces the first four):**
  1. A big question that speaks to the viewer, max 8 words, ending in "?" ("Paying only the minimum due?").
  2. The hero number at its final value and the tension line, with the key figure in [[highlighter]], all on screen at frame 0.
  3. Motion inside 0.5 s: the number settles from a slight pulse and the highlighter swipes across.
  4. Total length 15–20 s.
  5. No scene repeats what the hook already said.
- **Posting:** every reel goes out as a **Trial Reel** first. Share it to the profile only if its skip rate beats the previous reel's.
- **Scenes:** hook → units / fill → verdict. Fill headlines fit on one line (max 28 characters). → units / fill scenes → Night verdict → loop (the ₹ rebuilds so replays are seamless).
- **Signature moves:** pixel build, count-up with Indian digit grouping, squares filling one per unit, highlighter swipe, orange pixel-bar wipe between scenes, hard cut to Night for the verdict.
- **Never static:** slow 3.5% push-in on every scene.
- **Rhythm:** 84 bpm; scene changes on bar lines; words land a quarter-beat apart.
- **Easing:** quick in, gentle stop. No bounce, shake, 3D, stock footage, emoji or meme sounds.
- **Sound:** original lo-fi bed generated in code (no licensing issues) plus cues: pops as squares fill, ticks on count-ups, whoosh on wipes, thud on the verdict. Must still make sense on mute.
- **Voiceover:** free Kokoro voice "George" (British male), one short line per scene, numbers spelled out in words. Music ducks under the voice. Voiced reels may run to 25 s.
- **Colours:** same as BRAND.md: orange = the main story, blue = the alternative, grey = context, Night for the verdict only.
